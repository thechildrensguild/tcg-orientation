Getting started with GuildLink

Our intranet—policies, forms, recognition, and resources, all in one place.

Your link: thechildrensguild.sharepoint.com/sites/GuildLink2

Three ways to open it

On your computer
  1. Open Edge or Chrome.
  2. Go to the link above.
  3. Sign in with your work email.
  4. Bookmark it.
In Teams
  1. Open Microsoft Teams.
  2. Find GuildLink in the left rail—the GL icon.
  3. Click to open it inside Teams.
  4. It's pinned for everyone automatically.
On your phone
  1. Open Teams on your phone.
  2. Tap More (•••) at the bottom.
  3. Tap GuildLink.
  4. Or add a shortcut—see below.

Put it one tap away

On your phone

iPhone — Safari

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android — Chrome

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu, top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.
On your computer

Bookmark it in Edge

  1. Open the link in Edge.
  2. Click the star in the address bar.
  3. Choose Favorites bar, then Done.

Pin it to your taskbar

  1. Open the link in Edge.
  2. Click ••• → More tools → Pin to taskbar.
