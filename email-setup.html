Setting up your work email

Two apps, two short steps. Most people finish in about fifteen minutes.

First, download these two apps

Microsoft Outlook

Your work email. You'll use it every day to send and receive messages.

Microsoft Authenticator

Keeps your account secure. You'll use it to confirm it's really you when you sign in.

Both are free in the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).

Then follow these in order

On a laptop Start here
  1. Go to portal.office.com in a web browser.
  2. Sign in with your work email address and the temporary password from DP Solutions.
  3. Create a new password. Pick something strong you'll remember.
  4. Set up multi-factor authentication. Open Microsoft Authenticator on your phone and scan the QR code on the laptop screen.
  5. That's it—you're in. Have a look around your inbox.
On your phone
  1. Open the Microsoft Outlook app.
  2. Tap Add Account and enter your work email address.
  3. Enter the new password you just created.
  4. When prompted, open Microsoft Authenticator and approve the sign-in.
  5. Done. Your work email is on your phone.
Why start on a laptop? Changing your password and setting up authentication go more smoothly on a laptop. If you try both on your phone, the prompts appear on the same screen you're working from, which gets confusing. Start on the laptop, then add your phone.
